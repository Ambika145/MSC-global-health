About the Programme

Learn More →

The M.Sc. in Global Health & Human Development is an intensive, interdisciplinary online programme that equips learners to understand and tackle the complex dynamics of global health. The curriculum integrates public health science, epidemiology, health systems research, policy analysis, and human development theory. This provides students with a clear, research-driven framework to analyze health disparities and develop effective, sustainable interventions.

Taught by renowned faculty and global practitioners, the programme offers a flexible yet rigorous learning environment, making it ideal for recent graduates and established professionals alike. Through case-based learning and practical analytical work, students gain hands-on experience, graduating prepared to make meaningful contributions to public health organizations, development agencies, policy institutions, and the humanitarian sector.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I pay the fee in installments?

⌄
Yes. The fee is divided into a Block Amount and two installments for convenience.​

What is the application process?

⌄
Applicants must submit an online application along with their Statement of Purpose (SOP) and Curriculum Vitae (CV). Shortlisted candidates appear for an Aptitude Test.

Do I need a background in public health?

⌄
No. The programme welcomes applicants from diverse fields and provides a foundational grounding in global health and development.

How will I be evaluated for admission?

⌄
Admissions follow a holistic evaluation based on your SOP, CV, and Aptitude Test performance.

Who is eligible to apply?

⌄
A bachelor’s degree in any discipline from a recognised institution. Candidates from health, social sciences, development, sciences, or humanities backgrounds are encouraged to apply.

Is there an age limit for admission?

⌄
No specific age limit is mentioned. The programme is open to eligible learners from diverse backgrounds.
